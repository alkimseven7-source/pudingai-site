Who sees your conversations?

The window on the home page has no account. What you write stays in memory while you talk and isn't saved, so there is no record to read.

In the app (only in Turkish for now), you do. Your conversations are stored encrypted in your account and don't appear in the admin panel. We don't read your conversations. Your analyses, your map and your memory traces don't appear in the panel either.

Conversations aren't monitored in real time.

The operator sees your email address, your sign-up and last sign-in dates and your consent status, and reads what you write in the rating and feedback boxes and in the survey's free-text answer.

Where does it go?

What you write goes to Anthropic (USA) to generate replies, for safety checks and for analysis. Anthropic doesn't use it to train models. How long it's kept there, and what happens to content flagged for safety, is set out in Anthropic’s data terms. We can't delete it from there.

Phone numbers, email addresses, Turkish ID numbers, IBANs, card numbers and street addresses are replaced with a marker before they go. Names go as you wrote them.

The app runs in Frankfurt (Google Cloud) and records are kept in Dublin (Supabase). If you use voice typing, your voice goes to Google (EU). Emails are sent by Brevo (EU). In the home page window and on the sign-in and sign-up screen, Cloudflare checks your browser to filter out automated requests.

Your data isn't sold or used for advertising.

What is kept?

In the home page window, nothing: no account, and the conversation stays in memory and is dropped after 30 minutes of silence.

  • Open conversation: until you archive it. Archiving deletes the conversation.
  • Analysis and özüt (two words from the conversation): 365 days, the latest 30 conversations at most.
  • Memory traces and names you mention: 30 traces at most. You can turn remembering off.
  • Safety note: 90 days. It doesn't include what you wrote, only its type, the time, the helpline shown and the step taken.
  • Email address and date of birth: while your account exists. Your date of birth is encrypted; your email address and dates are not.

If you don't sign in for 365 days, your conversation and memory traces are deleted. Weekly encrypted backups are kept for 8 weeks, technical logs without content for 30 days.

Deleting and downloading

In the app's Options, you can delete a record, your whole archive or your account. Deleted items wait 7 days, and you can undo during that time. Then they're permanently deleted, and gone from backups within 8 weeks at most.

The one exception is safety notes: they can't be deleted and disappear on their own after 90 days.

You can also download your data from Options, using a code sent to your email. If you can't get in, write to kvkk@pudingai.com.

More detail

Every provider, every kind of data and every period is in the privacy notice (KVKK, in Turkish). Data controller: Enstitü Fabrika Etkinlik Organizasyon Ticaret Ltd. Şti. For requests and questions: kvkk@pudingai.com

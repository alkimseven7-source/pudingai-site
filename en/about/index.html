Puding AI

An AI that accompanies you through uncertainty.

MISSION

Puding helps people grow their capacity to stay with uncertainty — making sense of their movement without labeling it, and making visible the trail they leave.

VISION

A world where uncertainty is lived not as a lack but as a power, and where everyone carries the map of their own movement.

VALUES

What is, is. Experience is not an object waiting to be defined; it is an ongoing becoming.

Equivalence. Equivalence is difference: no two people are the same, and no position stands above another.

Consent. No definition is attached to a person; even when safety calls for it, we offer company, not labels.

Openness. Our theory will be published in open access; we explain openly what Puding does and why.

Self-critique. We turn the same scrutiny on our own concepts.
